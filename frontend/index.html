What is MoodShift?

MoodShift is your personal emotional wellness companion, designed to help you understand, track, and manage your moods with beautiful design and powerful insights.

📊

Track Your Moods

Log your emotional state with four distinct mood categories. Identify patterns and triggers in your daily life.

💭

Journal Deeply

Write detailed journal entries to reflect on your thoughts and feelings. Process your emotions through writing.

📈

Gain Insights

Access beautiful analytics and visualizations of your emotional trends. Make informed decisions about your wellness.

🤝

Connect & Share

Build a community with others on their emotional wellness journey. Share experiences and find support.
